# Feathers

**Huma Feathers** are the next evolution of our loyalty system, designed to reward community members based on their engagement with the Huma Protocol. Inspired by the mythical Huma bird, a symbol of prosperity and renewal, Feathers reflect the platform's agility, vision, and long-term alignment with its users.

All existing **Huma Points** have been rebranded as **Feathers,** converted at a 1:1 ratio. Points earned before the launch of Huma 2.0 remain accessible through the **Huma Institutional DApp**.

LPs can boost their Feathers earnings through several multiplier mechanisms:

- **Mode Multiplier** – Maxi Mode offers a higher multiplier than Classic Mode.
- **Lockup Multiplier** – Longer lockup periods result in higher multipliers.
- **OG LP Multiplier** – A lifetime bonus for LPs who participated before Huma 2.0 and remained active.
- **Loyal LP Multiplier** *(coming soon)* – For LPs who consistently retain their positions over time.
- **Community Boost Multiplier** – A temporary boost for users referred by partner communities.
- **ve Multiplier** *(coming soon)* – A rewards enhancement program modeled after the commitment-driven framework pioneered by Curve will be introduced, encouraging long-term participation.
