# Pools
## Active Pools

Currently, Huma offers a diverse range of pools, including:
- Arf: Cross-border payment settlement financing.
- Raincard: Secured business credit card platform.
- BSOS: Trade finance platform.
- Roam: DePIN financing.
- Jia: SMB financing

We focus on risk management to ensure the stability and security of our pools. Instead of expanding the number of pools, we focus on launching pools with relatively low risk.
