## Automatic Yield Payout and Reinvestment

At the end of each epoch, before closure, the yield accrued by you will either be automatically paid out or reinvested into the pool, depending on the settings. If you are receiving automatic yield distribution, the funds will be directly credited to your wallet, eliminating the need for manual withdrawal.

Note that this feature is currently only available in EVM pools where the setting has been enabled. It's not available in Solana or Stellar pools at the moment.
