# Multisig and Timelock Addresses

## Transparency Principles

Huma Protocol strives to operate at the highest level of transparency. We commit to abide by the following principles:

- The ownership of the Protocol Contracts, Huma Config, and Pool Contracts will be controlled by a timelock mechanism.
- Timelock operations will require a majority vote from a multisig wallet, with at least three signees.
- The timelock delay will be a minimum of 24 hours.
- All key addresses (including the timelock address and multisig address) for the protocol and all active pools are publicly available on this page.
